KALEboard Privacy
Privacy Policy for KALEboard
Last Updated: July 21, 2026

Welcome to KALEboard (“we,” “our,” or “us”). We are committed to protecting the privacy of all our users, especially children. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with the KALEboard mobile application (the “App”).

1. Zero Data Collection
KALEboard is designed as an offline-first, private Augmentative and Alternative Communication (AAC) tool.

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

We do not require account creation, registration, or login.

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, device identifiers, or IP addresses.

2. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)
Our App is designed for users of all ages, including children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). Because KALEboard does not collect any personal data from anyone, it is fully compliant with:

The United States Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

The European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) regarding children's data

Apple App Store’s Kids Category guidelines

Google Play Store’s Families Policy

We do not knowingly solicit or collect data from children under 13, nor do we track them.

3. On-Device Data Storage
Any custom boards, saved phrases, symbols, settings, or user preferences you create inside KALEboard are stored locally on your device only.

This data is never uploaded to external servers hosted by us.

If you back up your device using cloud services (such as Apple iCloud or Google Drive), your stored App settings may be included in your personal backup governed by Apple's or Google's privacy policies.

4. Third-Party Services, Analytics & Ads
To ensure total privacy and a child-safe environment:

No Advertising: KALEboard contains no third-party advertisements or ad networks.

No Analytics/Tracking: We do not use third-party analytics SDKs, tracking pixels, or telemetry frameworks to monitor user behavior.

No Third-Party Data Sharing: We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any user data with third parties.

5. Device Permissions
KALEboard may request access to certain hardware features strictly necessary for core AAC functionality (such as text-to-speech output or custom audio recording/board customization):

Microphone / Media Access (if applicable): Used strictly on-device to record custom audio prompts or upload custom images to your boards. No recorded audio, images, or media files ever leave your device.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in legal requirements or App functionality. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
